At most contractors the workforce plan that actually works lives in one person’s head, and everyone in the office knows whose. Jamie Miller, Director of Engineering Development at Sellen Construction, described the spreadsheet version. She spent hours building forecasts and then explaining to everyone how to read them, “because it’s only in my brain”.

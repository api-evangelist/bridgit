Bridgit announced expansion to specialty contractors with two new capabilities: Labor Forecasting and Crew Management. The platform, trusted by 40% of ENR 400 contractors, now addresses the crew-centric demands of specialty trades. Labor Forecasting enables visual modeling of labor demand across projects, while Crew Management lets teams manage their workforce as operational units.
